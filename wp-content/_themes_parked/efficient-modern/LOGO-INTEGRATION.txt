🔧 TO CUSTOMIZE THE LOGO:

METHOD 1: WordPress Customizer (Recommended)
1. Go to: Appearance → Customize → Site Identity
2. Click "Select Logo"
3. Upload your logo
4. Adjust crop if needed
5. Publish

METHOD 2: Options Framework (If installed)
1. Go to: Appearance → Theme Options
2. Find "Logo" field
3. Upload/Select your logo
4. Find "Footer Logo" field (for footer)
5. Save Options

METHOD 3: Replace Default (Quick)
1. Replace: /assets/images/logo.png
2. Keep same filename
3. Recommended size: 200-300px height
4. Format: PNG with transparency

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

💡 LOGO BEST PRACTICES:

Recommended Specifications:
- Format: PNG with transparent background
- Height: 200-300px (will scale to 50px in header)
- Width: Proportional to your logo
- File Size: Under 100KB for fast loading
- Color: Works on white background (header/footer)

If your logo has white text/elements:
- Consider adding a subtle outline or shadow
- Or use a version with darker elements
- Current burgundy theme works with most logos
