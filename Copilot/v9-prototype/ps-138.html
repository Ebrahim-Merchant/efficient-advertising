Technical Specifications

Materials, Finishes & Options

All specifications below are standard options — custom substrates available on request.

White Sheet (Top)The top writing sheet. Printed in full colour with your logo, fields, and numbering. This is the sheet the customer or user writes on.
Yellow Sheet (Copy 1)First carbon copy — standard in both 2-part and 3-part configurations. Used for your internal records or customer receipt.
Pink Sheet (Copy 2)Second carbon copy — included in 3-part forms only. Used for a third party such as accounts, store, or warehouse.
Standard NCRClean, press-sensitive carbonless paper with no perforation. Forms are supplied as loose sets or glued pads in books of 25, 50, or 100.
Perforated NCREach form has a perforation line for easy tear-out. Ideal for delivery notes, work orders, and any field application requiring clean single-sheet separation.
Retail & WholesaleSales invoices, purchase orders, and goods received notes where instant duplicates are required at the point of transaction.
Contractors & TradespeopleJob cards, quotations, and work completion forms — 3-part lets one copy stay with the client, one with accounts, one with the field team.
Logistics & DeliveryDelivery notes accompanying shipments. Driver retains one copy, recipient signs and keeps a copy, dispatcher files the original.
RestaurantsBill books, table order pads, and kitchen dockets. NCR eliminates the need for re-writing orders for kitchen and front-of-house.
Service BusinessesService reports, inspection forms, and maintenance records for businesses requiring multi-copy paper trails for compliance or customer sign-off.

Assembly & Installation

Set Up in Minutes

Follow these steps to assemble and install your product. No tools required for most configurations.

01

Press Firmly When Writing

Write with a ballpoint pen on the top sheet using firm, even pressure. The carbonless coating transfers your writing to all sheets simultaneously.

02

Separate Sheets After Writing

Tear or perforate (if ordered) the completed set cleanly. Give the copy sheet(s) to the relevant party and retain the original.

03

File for Records

Your top (white) sheet is the original record. File it sequentially by number or date. The sequence number helps with cross-referencing and audits.

04

Reorder Before You Run Out

Keep a buffer of at least one book in stock. NCR forms take 3–5 days to produce — contact us early to avoid running out mid-job.
